TITLE: “Pacifican Sovereignty Initiative Amendment”

We the People of [State/Province], recognizing our unalienable rights to self-government, regional determination, and political autonomy, do hereby amend our Constitution to authorize and obligate our State/Province Government to initiate, facilitate, and support the peaceful creation of a voluntary confederation of sovereign communities under the name “Pacifica.”. This shall be executed through lawful, popular representation, and constitutional means.

The policy of the State of [State/Province] shall be to:
1. Pursue participation in a federated Pacifican confederation grounded in natural rights, local autonomy, and direct democratic governance.
2. Recognize the right of the People to withdraw from external governance when that governance no longer serves liberty, justice, or ecological balance.
3. Seek an open, peaceful transition of powers through democratic consent and international legal norms.

Within 12 months of this amendment’s ratification, the government of [State/Province] shall:

1. Issue a public referendum asking:

Whether this State/Province shall formally participate in a constitutional convention to consider exiting our membership with our motherland.

Whether the People approve the currently proposed Draft Constitution of Pacifica and Sovereign Persons, or desire to: a) Propose line vetos or amendments thereto, or
b) Draft a new regional constitutional synthesis.

2. Provide for full public education, open hearings, and access to foundational documents at no cost.

Upon passage of this amendment:

1. The State Gubernor or Province Premier, including also the Legislature, and all relevant agencies shall be mandated to take all legal and peaceful actions necessary to:
Facilitate a Pacifican constitutional referendum and convention.
Establish dialogue with Pacifican delegates from other states and territories.
Negotiate in good faith toward secession.

1. The State shall provide public support for citizen assemblies, open conventions, and civic technology to enable direct democratic participation in the Pacifica transition process.

Participation in Pacifica shall not preclude continued relations or dual identity within the United States, Canada, Mexico, or other legal bodies unless and until formal independence is achieved through recognized processes and agreements. The rights of all indigenous nations and treaty holders shall remain fully protected and respected.

This amendment shall take effect immediately upon certification by the appropriate electoral authority following its ratification through the constitutional process. The State/Province shall prioritize its implementation over all conflicting obligations.
